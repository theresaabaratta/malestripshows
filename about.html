About This Guide

This site is a small, independent guide based in Charleston, SC, built to track the traveling male revues that occasionally book dates in the area — where they show up, who they are, and what to expect.

We're not a venue, a booking agency, or affiliated with any of the touring groups listed here. Everything on this site is informational, sourced from what these traveling acts publish about their own tours.

Tour schedules change often, so always confirm dates, venues, and tickets directly with the group or venue before making plans.
